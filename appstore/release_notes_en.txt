What's new in version 1.3.0:

• Choose your preferred units per parameter
• Temperature in °C or °F
• Salinity as specific gravity or ppt
• Alkalinity in dKH, meq/L, or ppm CaCO₃
• Nitrate as ppm NO3 or ppm NO₃-N
• Phosphate as ppm or ppb (Hanna ULR)
• Defaults, steppers, thresholds, charts, and history all adapt to your chosen units
• Stored values stay canonical so CSV backups remain portable
